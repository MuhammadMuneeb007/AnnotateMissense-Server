AnnotateMissense

AnnotateMissense: a genome-wide annotation and benchmarking framework for missense pathogenicity prediction. Muhammad Muneeb and David B. Ascher.

Pipeline repository Server repository Zenodo release Documentation

1 · Annotation

90,643,830 GRCh38 missense SNVs (dbNSFP v5.1, CAGI7) annotated with ANNOVAR gene-, region- and filter-based databases (dbNSFP v4.2a/c, gnomAD v2.1.1/v3.0, ClinVar, dbSNP…) and merged with AlphaMissense and ESM1v predictions.

2 · Benchmark

132,714 ClinVar P/LP vs B/LB variants; stratified five-fold CV. XGBoost on the full feature set: mean MCC 0.9411, ROC-AUC 0.9950; genomic-allele-grouped CV MCC 0.9380. Circularity-controlled ablations quantify reliance on prior predictors.

3 · Genome-wide inference

A genome-wide XGBoost classifier (1,044 input dimensions) scored every allele. Temporal validation on 298,353 newer ClinVar variants: MCC 0.7613, accuracy 0.8798.
